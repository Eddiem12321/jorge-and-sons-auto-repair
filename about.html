A family shop since 1998

Jorge Ramírez came to New Jersey from Puebla, Mexico, in 1989 with a suitcase and his father’s wrenches.

He grew up fixing tractors and taxis in his father’s garage. In New Jersey, he spent nine years at shops in Newark and Irvington, learning every car that came through the door and saving what he could.

In 1998 he rented a single bay on Hollister Lane and painted the sign himself. His wife, Rosa, kept the books at the kitchen table.

Luis and Miguel grew up in the shop.

Luis was changing oil by twelve and now runs the service desk as an ASE-certified technician. Miguel handles engines and transmissions. Jorge still opens the doors at 7:30 every morning.

Four bays now, and the same promise we started with.
